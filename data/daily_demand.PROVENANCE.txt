daily_demand.csv: REAL. Daily electricity demand in GWh, 1 Jan 2022 - 22 Aug 2026 (1,695 days, no gaps), PJM
Interconnection (United States), from the US EIA Hourly Electric Grid Monitor (EIA-930 per-BA file). Hourly demand with
EIA's imputed values where flagged, summed to local days; three fall-DST days with one empty duplicated-hour row scaled
to their true 25-hour length; trailing partial day dropped. Strong weekly and annual seasonality with US holiday effects
(use US federal holidays for calendar features). An ERCOT equivalent is available from the instructor. Downloaded
24 Aug 2026.
